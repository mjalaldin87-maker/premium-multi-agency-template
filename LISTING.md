# Product Listing — Your Brand Multi-Purpose Agency Website Template

## Short summary
A flexible multi-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript website starter for agencies, creative studios, consultants, and service businesses.
Includes a four-style live brand preview, reusable page layouts, and CSS/SVG artwork you can adapt to your own identity.

## What you get
- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript source files; no build step
- Homepage with four live brand styles
- Services, work, case-study, about, pricing, contact, thank-you, and 404 pages
- Customizer and niche starter page
- Setup documentation, changelog, and QA checklist
- CSS/SVG concept artwork without stock-photo dependencies
- Draft licence and policy-page starters

## What you do not get
- Hosting, a domain, or a custom email address
- A configured Formspree account or working form ID
- Bundled Bricolage Grotesque or Instrument Sans font files
- Custom business copy, real portfolio projects, team photos, or professional legal advice
- Guaranteed sales, leads, rankings, revenue, or performance scores

## FAQs
1. **Do I need a build tool?** No. The template is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
2. **Can I change the brand colours?** Yes. The homepage and customizer demonstrate four CSS-variable brand styles. Editing the live preview does not rewrite source files.
3. **Does the contact form work immediately?** No. You must configure your own Formspree form ID and test the redirect.
4. **Can I use it for client projects?** The intended licence allows personal and client projects after purchase, subject to the completed final licence.
5. **Are all pages ready to publish as-is?** No. Replace placeholders, sample concepts, contact details, domain values, and legal text first.
6. **Are fonts and hosting included?** No. Font binaries are not bundled, and hosting/domain costs are separate.
