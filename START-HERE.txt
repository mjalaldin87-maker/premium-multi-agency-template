PREMIUM MULTI-AGENCY WEBSITE TEMPLATE — START HERE

Get the template running in three steps.

1. OPEN
Download and extract the ZIP. Open index.html in a modern browser. No build tools or package installation are required.

2. PREVIEW AND CUSTOMIZE
Open customizer.html and try the four brand styles. Replace "Your Brand" with your business name. Replace the sample copy and every [FILL IN] prompt with accurate information before publishing.

3. TEST AND PUBLISH
Configure your own Formspree form ID in contact.html, replace the placeholder domain and contact details, and test a real submission and the thank-you redirect. Check every page on desktop and mobile before deploying.

IMPORTANT
The contact form intentionally blocks submission while the YOUR_FORM_ID placeholder is present, instead of pretending to send an enquiry. It will not receive enquiries until you connect your own form provider. Never include your private form ID or API secret in a public template. Two fonts are bundled locally with their SIL Open Font License notices. The licence, privacy notice, terms, refund policy, and support policy are starter material: review and complete them for your own circumstances before publishing or selling a customized site.
