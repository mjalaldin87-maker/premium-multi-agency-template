COMMON QUESTIONS

The details
that matter.

Why don’t you list fixed prices?

Scope can vary significantly by business, deliverables, and complexity. A real agency should publish clear starting prices where possible and provide a written quote once requirements are understood.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. Engagements can be shaped around in-house marketers, developers, founders, and other specialists, with responsibilities agreed at the start.

What happens after launch?

Handover and ongoing support should be defined in the proposal. Maintenance, new features, and retained creative work can be scoped separately.
