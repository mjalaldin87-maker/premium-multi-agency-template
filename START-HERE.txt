YOUR BRAND — START HERE

Get the template running in three steps.

1. OPEN
Download the repository ZIP and extract it. Open index.html in a modern browser. No build tools or package installation are required.

2. PREVIEW AND CUSTOMIZE
Open customizer.html and try the four brand styles. Search the files for "Your Brand" and replace it with your business name. Replace all [FILL IN] prompts and demo concept content before publishing.

3. TEST AND PUBLISH
Set up your own Formspree form ID in contact.html, replace the placeholder domain, then test the form and thank-you redirect. Check all pages on desktop and mobile before deploying.

IMPORTANT
The form is not ready to receive enquiries until you configure Formspree. Font binaries are not bundled yet; system fallbacks are used. The licence, privacy notice, terms, refund policy, and support policy must be reviewed and completed before sale.
