PREMIUM MULTI-AGENCY WEBSITE TEMPLATE — START HERE

Get the template running in three steps.

1. OPEN
Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a modern browser. No build tools or package installation are required.

2. PREVIEW AND CUSTOMIZE
Open customizer.html to preview the four brand styles. Replace "Your Brand" with your business name. Replace sample copy and every [FILL IN] prompt with accurate information before publishing.

3. TEST AND PUBLISH
In contact.html, replace YOUR_FORM_ID with your own Formspree form ID. Update the redirect to your real domain and thank-you.html. Submit a real test enquiry and confirm it arrives. Check every page on desktop and mobile before deploying.

IMPORTANT
The contact form intentionally blocks submission while YOUR_FORM_ID is still present. It will not receive enquiries until you connect your own form provider. Never include private API secrets in a public template.

LICENCE AND POLICY PAGES
LICENSE.txt explains the template-use licence. The privacy.html and terms.html pages are starter templates, not legal advice or ready-made legal policies. Edit them to accurately reflect your own business, data practices, services, and applicable law before publishing. The product seller's checkout, refund, and support terms are separate from the policies you create for your own website.

FONTS
The fonts folder includes Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans with SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices. Keep those notices with the font files.

FINAL CHECK
Search all files for [FILL IN], YOUR_FORM_ID, yourdomain.com, and sample business details. Replace each relevant placeholder, test every page and link, and confirm the site works at mobile and desktop sizes before launch.
